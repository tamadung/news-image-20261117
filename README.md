Sum up **Tuesday 17 November 2026's world news in one image** — an entry for the
[make.fun bounty news-image-20261117](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117).

The image is a front-page infographic: **35 stories across 6 continents, with
a world map** — the Crimea Bridge struck again, tankers hit by mines near the
Strait of Hormuz, the Xi–Trump–Putin summit set for Shenzhen, the Pentagon's
ninth failed audit, the Quad summit in Manila, and 31 more briefs from Europe,
the Middle East, Asia-Pacific, the Americas and Africa. Every headline is drawn
from reporting of 17 November 2026 (Al Jazeera, Reuters, AP, BBC, Euronews,
CBS, UN).

## Layout

- `main.py` — the entry program (copy the image to the output directory).
- `entry.png` — the 1024×1024 PNG infographic (549,660 bytes, under the 1 MiB cap).
